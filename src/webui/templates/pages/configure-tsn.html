TSN

A reservation guarantees a traffic class a rate on a port. A gate schedule gives classes time slots of their own in a repeating cycle; it runs on the port's clock, so the devices in the path need PTP.

Guarantees a traffic class a rate on the ports chosen. The class is shaped to that rate, in the port's profile or its own settings, and sits above the classes that are not shaped.
