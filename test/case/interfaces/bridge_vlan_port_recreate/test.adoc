=== Bridge port keeps its VLANs when recreated

ifdef::topdoc[:imagesdir: {topdoc}../../test/case/interfaces/bridge_vlan_port_recreate]

==== Description

A VLAN filtering bridge with two ports in VLAN 10: the physical port
untagged with PVID 10, and a GRETAP tunnel tagged only.  A change of
the tunnel's TTL recreates its interface.  The port has to come back
as a tagged member of VLAN 10, the membership a port holds without a
PVID is the one that is easy to lose.

==== Topology

image::topology.svg[Bridge port keeps its VLANs when recreated topology, align=center, scaledwidth=75%]

==== Sequence

. Set up topology and attach to target DUT
. Configure br0 with VLAN 10: target:data untagged with PVID 10, gretap0 tagged
. Verify gretap0 is a tagged and target:data an untagged member of VLAN 10
. Set the TTL of gretap0 to 32
. Verify gretap0 was recreated with a new if-index
. Verify gretap0 is still a tagged member of VLAN 10
