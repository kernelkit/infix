=== QoS Egress Shaper

ifdef::topdoc[:imagesdir: {topdoc}../../test/case/interfaces/qos_egress_shaper]

==== Description

One talker, bridged through the DUT and out an egress port shaped to
10 Mbit/s, offering three times that.  What arrives at the listener in
the middle seconds of the flow must be the shaped rate, within a fifth,
whatever the priority of the flow: once marked EF, in a strict class
near the top, once marked CS1, in the lowest class.  Then both at once,
each offering one and a half times the rate, must still add up to the
rate.

A shaper that lets more through is not a shaper, and one that only bites
on some queues is a scheduler fault dressed up as one.  The test's own
iperf3 control traffic, DSCP 0, rides with CS1 in the lowest loaded
class, so a talker's end-of-test message leaves the port behind its last
datagram.

==== Sequence

. Set up topology and attach to target DUT
. Bridge the two ports, trust DSCP on ingress, shape the egress port
. Set up the talker and listener namespaces
. One EF flow at three times the limit, expect the limit through
. One CS1 flow at three times the limit, expect the limit through
. Both at one and a half times the limit, expect the limit through in total
